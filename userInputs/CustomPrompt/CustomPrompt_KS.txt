EMPLOYEE BUSINESS EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

1. PURPOSE

This policy outlines the procedures for submitting and reimbursing business expenses incurred by employees on behalf of [Company Name]. The objective is to ensure financial prudence and compliance.

2. GENERAL GUIDELINES

A. Necessity: All reimbursed expenses must be necessary, reasonable, and directly related to company business. B. Approval: Prior written approval from an immediate supervisor is required for any single expense exceeding $500 USD. C. Documentation: Original or digital receipts are mandatory for all expenses. Expenses without valid documentation will not be reimbursed. D. Timeliness: Expense reports must be submitted within 15 calendar days of the expense date or within 30 calendar days of completing a business trip.

3. TRAVEL AND TRANSPORTATION

A. Air Travel: Employees must book Economy Class flights. Business Class requires VP-level approval. B. Lodging: The maximum nightly limit for accommodation, including taxes and fees, is $250 USD for domestic travel and $350 USD for international major cities. Itemized hotel invoices are required. C. Mileage: Reimbursement for use of a personal vehicle is calculated at the IRS Standard Rate per mile, based on the logged official mileage. Tolls and parking are reimbursable separately with receipts.

4. MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT

A. Individual Meal Per Diem: The daily maximum limit for individual meals while traveling is $75 USD per day. B. Client Entertainment: The limit is $150 USD per person (including the employee). C. Documentation: For client entertainment, the receipt must list the names of attendees and the business purpose discussed. Alcohol expenses must not exceed 30% of the total meal cost.

5. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

The following expenses will NOT be reimbursed:

Personal items (e.g., toiletries, clothing).

In-flight entertainment or premium Wi-Fi upgrades.

Traffic violations, parking tickets, or late fees.

Spouse, family, or pet travel costs.

Gift cards or personal gifts to clients without prior HR approval.

6. SUBMISSION PROCEDURE

All expenses must be logged and submitted using the company's designated expense reporting system, [Name of Expense System]. Approved reports are typically processed for payment within 5 business days.
